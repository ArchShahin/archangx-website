Products · Seraphim Guardian

Seraphim Guardian: The Eternal Watch

An orbital AI intelligence platform for detecting, classifying and reporting critical thermal and aerospace events from satellite observations, with secure data processing and mission-specific AI. Two products share one platform: Guardian Uriel watches for the first spark of wildfire; Guardian Aurora studies vigilance beyond the northern horizon.

Guardian UrielGuardian Aurora
The name

Why Seraphim, why Guardian

In the old tradition the Seraphim are the watchers closest to the light, and Uriel is the archangel of light, wisdom and fire. The names describe a posture, not a technology: a constant watch from above, in the service of life below. What does the watching is thermal physics, sensors, satellites and software, and nothing on these pages claims more than that.

Mission statement

The Eternal Watch

From the first spark upon the Earth to the distant horizons of the Arctic sky, Seraphim Guardian envisions a future where celestial intelligence serves awareness, protection and the preservation of life.

Guardian runs on the Seraphim space data centre: the observations are processed where they are captured, so what reaches the ground is a signed, geolocated event, not a raw image.

Two products

One watch, two horizons

Product · Wildfire intelligence

Guardian Uriel

Celestial Intelligence Protecting Life on Earth.

AI-powered wildfire ignition detection and early warning. Thermal and multispectral Earth observation, physics-informed anomaly detection, onboard AI inference at the satellite edge, confidence scoring and geolocation, event visualization and alert delivery, and integration with the emergency management organizations that act on an alert.

Intelligence that sees the first spark → Research module · Conceptual

Guardian Aurora

The Northern Light of Eternal Vigil.

A proposed future research and development module on Arctic aerospace situational awareness: what satellite observation of thermal signatures in the northern polar regions would require in sensors, coverage, latency, data integrity and governance, and how early-warning information could be delivered securely. Conceptual and non-operational, demonstrated only with synthetic data.

Shared platform

One platform, two missions, kept apart

Both products reuse the orbital data platform below the line. Above it, each mission has its own datasets, models, access policies, validation and approvals. Civilian and defence-related workloads are never mixed without explicit authorization and an approved data-classification policy.

Guardian UrielCivilian wildfire operations: fire datasets and models, agency integrations, public alert formats
Guardian AuroraConceptual research environment: synthetic data only, separate access policy, no operational interfaces
