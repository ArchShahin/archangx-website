About

Shahin Vakilinia

Shahin Vakilinia, PhD, leads the Space Data Center programme at ArchAngX Corp. He has worked at nearly every layer a space data centre is made of: edge GPUs, quantum computing platforms, cloud data centres, 5G networks, satellites, data platforms and risk models. Seraphim is where they meet.

Biography

Building the space data centre

Shahin's work connects three things that are usually designed apart: the technology stack of a data centre in orbit, a digital twin that models it, and the machine-learning wildfire detection that runs on it.

He is the author of the technical work behind this site, including an orbital data and AI platform for autonomous wildfire detection, the coupled digital twin of the space data centre, and the architecture that keeps its agents secure, compliant and accountable.

Before Seraphim, Shahin spent about three years at Nutrien building with NVIDIA edge GPUs, the same class of hardware that has to compute on board a satellite. At Ericsson he worked on 5G and the automation of cloud data centres. He designed risk models as Risk Model Architect at EQ Bank, and data systems as a data architect for the public sector at Invest in Canada. He has also worked with Telesat on satellite communications, with Telus on Google Cloud Platform, and at PwC.

He studied electrical and computer engineering for his bachelor's and master's degrees, then earned a PhD at Concordia University (2011–2015) on modelling and optimizing dynamic resource allocation in cloud data centres. He has published many peer-reviewed papers on cloud resource allocation, energy-efficient data centres and service-level guarantees.

The dream

An architect for space data centres

Frank Gehry, born in Toronto, gave the world buildings no one had imagined before. Shahin's dream is to be that kind of architect for data centres in space: to design the places in orbit where the world's data is computed, and to build them from Canada.

Proud Canadian

Built in Canada, for Canada and the world

Shahin is a proud Canadian. Seraphim's first mission is wildfire detection for Canada's forests and the North, with a Canada-first plan for its ground segment and connectivity.
