About

Shahin Vakilinia

Shahin Vakilinia leads the Space Data Center programme at ArchAngX Corp., working with the Space Data Center team on orbital data platforms for wildfire intelligence.

Biography

Building the space data centre

Shahin's work connects three things that are usually designed apart: the technology stack of a data centre in orbit, a digital twin that models it, and the machine-learning wildfire detection that runs on it.

He is the author of the technical work behind this site, including an orbital data and AI platform for autonomous wildfire detection, the coupled digital twin of the space data centre, and the architecture that keeps its agents secure, compliant and accountable.

Areas of work

What he works on

Orbital data and AI platforms

Edge processing in orbit, multi-modal Earth observation and heterogeneous, hybrid quantum-classical compute.

Wildfire detection

Physics-informed thermal anomaly scoring and onboard processing that produce geolocated fire events.

Digital twins

Coupled orbit, power, thermal, compute and network models, fault injection and resilience analysis.

Assured agent operations

Agent platforms with zero-trust security, layered inspection and reporting, and continuous compliance on the cloud.
