Seraphim Guardian · Research module

Guardian Aurora: vigilance beyond the northern horizon

The Northern Light of Eternal Vigil. A proposed future research and development module on Arctic aerospace situational awareness: what it would take to observe critical thermal events over the northern polar regions from space, and to deliver early-warning information securely to those authorized to act on it.

Conceptual and non-operationalGuardian Aurora is a research concept. It does not track targets, estimate launch locations, guide interceptors or support engagement, and it makes no detection-performance claims. Every demonstration uses synthetic, non-sensitive data.
Objectives

What the research module studies

Aurora asks the questions that come before any system: whether, how and under what governance the Seraphim platform could support early warning in the Arctic.

  • Study satellite-based detection of missile-launch thermal signatures in northern polar regions
  • Explore the requirements for monitoring hypersonic threats
  • Develop a high-level concept for secure early-warning information delivery
  • Assess sensor, coverage, latency, data-integrity and governance requirements
  • Provide non-operational visual demonstrations using synthetic data
  • Support research into resilient satellite observation and event provenance
Conceptual workflow

From observation to protected information

Each stage is a boundary. Analysis is confined to the mission, every event is reviewed and validated by authorized people before it becomes information, and the information service is protected end to end.

Space-based observations→Sensor data-quality assessment→Secure event ingestion→ Mission-specific analysis boundary→Authorized review and validation→Protected early-warning information service
Study areas

What has to be understood first

Environment

Arctic and orbital constraints

Polar coverage from inclined and polar orbits, months of twilight and darkness, auroral and ionospheric effects on sensing and links, and cold, cloud and sea-ice backgrounds.

Sensing

General sensing and coverage

Which thermal bands separate a brief, intense event from its background; what revisit and latency a warning would need; how many satellites a continuous watch implies. Stated as requirements, not as achieved performance.

Assurance

Security, provenance and information assurance

Events signed at the source, integrity checked at every hop, tamper-evident logs, and resilience to interference, spoofing and loss of links or satellites.

Governance

Human authorization and operational governance

No automated consequence. Every event passes authorized human review and validation; roles, approvals, retention and oversight are defined before any data flows.

Research gaps

Hypersonic threat warning

Open questions on signature persistence, manoeuvring flight, false-alarm suppression and the latency budget of a warning. Catalogued as gaps for research, not as planned features.

Separation

Apart from civilian wildfire operations

Separate datasets, models, access policies, environments and approvals from Guardian Uriel. Shared infrastructure is used only under an approved data-classification policy.

Digital twin

Arctic-centred demonstration

In the Seraphim digital twin, Aurora is an Arctic-centred view with conceptual satellite observation coverage, a synthetic warning-event lifecycle, and the data provenance and authorized-review status of each event. There are no realistic trajectories, targeting solutions or interception calculations.

What it is not

Boundaries

  • Not an operational missile-warning capability
  • No target tracking, interception guidance or launch-location estimation
  • No engagement functionality of any kind
  • No detection-performance claims without verified evidence
