Tickets for Seraphim Summit 2027

One ticket covers one edition, Toronto or Montreal: you choose the city at checkout. Payment is handled by Stripe on a secure, Stripe-hosted page; ArchangX never sees your card details. Prices are in Canadian dollars, plus applicable taxes.

How it works

Three steps, no account needed

  1. Choose a pass

    Pick the ticket type below and the number of people (up to ten per order).

  2. Pay on Stripe

    Checkout opens on buy.stripe.com. Choose your edition, enter the attendee names and pay by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay.

  3. Keep the receipt

    Stripe emails your receipt at once; it is your proof of purchase. We email the practical details about two weeks before the event.

Privacy

What we receive when you buy

Payment is processed by Stripe Payments Canada, Ltd. on Stripe's servers; ArchangX never sees or stores your card number. From each order we receive your name, email address, billing address, organisation, the edition you chose, the names of any other attendees, any dietary or accessibility needs you enter, and the order amount.

How it is used

Only for this event

We use these details to run the event: badges, check-in, catering, accessibility arrangements and practical emails about the event you bought a ticket for. We do not sell or share them, and we do not add you to a marketing list without a separate opt-in. Data is kept until 90 days after the event, except what tax law requires us to keep. Under Québec's Law 25 and PIPEDA you can ask to see, correct or delete your data: shahin.vakilinia@archangx.com. Stripe's own notice: stripe.com/en-ca/privacy.
