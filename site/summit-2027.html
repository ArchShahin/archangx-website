Toronto · 21–22 June 2027

Seraphim Summit: data centres in orbit

Two days on the stack that puts data centres in orbit and the wildfire mission that proves it: a live digital twin demonstration, eight panels, an exhibition hall and a hackathon. Venue to be announced.

Register

Opening

National Indigenous Peoples Day

21 June is National Indigenous Peoples Day. We open with a welcome and land acknowledgement from an Indigenous fire-stewardship leader, on the communities that live on the front line of wildfire.

09:00 · Opening keynote

Seraphim, a protocol stack for data centres in orbit

Shahin Vakilinia, ArchangX. Live on the digital twin: a fire ignites, the satellite detects it, a 149-byte alert reaches the ground in about a minute and a half, and the agents explain every step.

Day 1 · Monday 21 June

Eight panels

Conference and exhibition. Speakers will be announced as they confirm.

Panel 1

Qubits in Orbit

Where quantum computing helps space data centres and where classical solvers already win. Post-quantum cryptography on hardware whose keys can never be replaced, and quantum key distribution over optical links between satellites.

Panel 2

From Space Weather to Sovereignty

The risk register for an orbital data centre: geomagnetic storms, radiation and silent data corruption, debris, launch, and data residency during a storm reroute. Loss modelling, insurance and who pays.

Panel 3

Data Platforms in Orbit

From Kafka to the lakehouse when the network doesn't work: event streaming, delay-tolerant bridges, lakehouse tables, catalogues, caching, governance and lineage.

Panel 4

Power, Heat and Silicon

Radiation-tolerant GPUs and TPUs, high-voltage DC and solid-state conversion, thermal buffers and radiators, optical terminals, and what terrestrial AI data centres can teach space.

Panel 5

Macro-Agents and Skills

How a few people can run hundreds of satellites with agentic AI that observes and recommends but never commands, and how zero trust applies to the agents themselves.

Panel 6

After Kubernetes

Orchestrating clusters that lose contact for an hour: WebAssembly, unikernels, edge Kubernetes, delay-aware and energy-aware scheduling, and GitOps without a reachable control plane.

Panel 7

AI Sovereignty, 6G and Self-Organizing Networks

Who controls data, models and networks when compute moves to orbit: sovereign ground cores, non-terrestrial 6G, self-healing networks, and Seraphim's Canada-first connectivity plan, starting in Saskatchewan.

Panel 8

Fire, From Lightning to War

Onboard wildfire detection, early warning in the North and a global fire index aiming at zero uncontrolled fire. Fire that is seen cannot be hidden.
