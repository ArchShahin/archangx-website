What is Seraphim

Cloud infrastructure, put in orbit.

Seraphim is ArchangX's platform for running compute and storage in orbit. Data is processed where it is collected, in space. It is linked to sovereign ground infrastructure, and connected to terrestrial and 5G/6G non-terrestrial networks.

At the summit we show how it works, who it is for, and what the road from first launch to production looks like. COPY DRAFT — tech/legal review
